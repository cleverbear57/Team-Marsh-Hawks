Welcome, California grower 👋

Rotation Compass helps you explore whether a different crop rotation could build healthier soil, use less irrigation water and ride out dry years — using NASA satellite records for your area, your soil, and your priorities. It is a thinking tool: it supports your judgement and your local farm advisor, and does not replace either.

About two minutes: pick your region, crop and top priority, set a timeline, then launch the simulation.

1Where is your field?

Pick a California growing region, or enter your own coordinates (we match the nearest regional profile and you can pull live NASA POWER climate for the exact point later).

2What do you grow now?

Your current (baseline) crop becomes Strategy A — the benchmark everything is compared against.

3What matters most to you?

Your top goal gets the most weight when we rank the strategies. The other goals still count.
